About Bazaar Lane

Bazaar Lane is a marketplace for small independent shops: pickle makers, potters, weavers and craftspeople. Each shop runs its own corner here, with its own products, prices, stock and shipping. You fill one bag from as many shops as you like and pay once.

Why we started

Good hand-made goods are hard to find online, and small makers rarely have time to run a website. Bazaar Lane gives them a shop in a few minutes and gives buyers one place to find them.

How we earn

Shops pay us a commission on each sale they deliver. Buyers pay no extra fee to us: the price you see is the shop's price, plus that shop's shipping.

Who checks the shops

Every new shop is checked by our team before it can sell. Shops that break our seller guidelines can be suspended, and any listing can be reported.
