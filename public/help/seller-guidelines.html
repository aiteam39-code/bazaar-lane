Seller guidelines

What you may sell

Things you make, grow or prepare yourself, or source honestly from small makers: food you are licensed to sell, home goods, clothing, crafts and gifts.

What you may not sell

  • Anything illegal, stolen, counterfeit or copying another brand's name or design.
  • Weapons, drugs, medicines, tobacco, alcohol, live animals, or anything unsafe.
  • Food without the licence and labels the law asks for.

Honest listings

Use your own photos of the real product on a plain background. Say what it is made of, its size and weight, and how to use or care for it. Keep your stock right: do not list what you cannot send.

Shipping on time

Accept or reject new orders quickly, and send within the dispatch days you set. Orders that pass their "Ship by" date show as LATE to you and to our team. Always add the courier and tracking number.

Reviews and reports

Only buyers with a delivered order can review, once per product per order. You can reply once to each review: be polite and helpful. Never ask for, buy or write reviews. Anyone can report a listing; our team may hide it or suspend the shop.
