Privacy

What we keep

When you order: your name, phone number, email address and delivery address, and what you ordered. When you sell: your shop's details, your email and phone. We do not keep card numbers: the payment company handles those.

Why

To deliver your order (each shop sees the name, phone and address for its own part only), to send you emails about it, to handle refunds and help requests, and to keep the marketplace safe.

How long

Order details are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require, usually up to 8 years. Newsletter sign-ups are kept until you unsubscribe, which you can do from any newsletter email.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your details, write to us through the contact form. We answer within 2 working days.

Cookies

Buyers get no tracking cookies. Sellers and staff get one login cookie that ends after 8 hours. Your bag, your saved products and your theme choice are kept only in your own browser.
