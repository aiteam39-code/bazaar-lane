Questions and answers

Shipping

Why do I pay shipping more than once?

Each shop sends its own parcel, so each shop charges its own shipping. Many shops ship free when your items from them come to more than their limit; your bag shows how much more you need.

When will it arrive?

Every product page shows an estimate: the shop's dispatch days plus 2 to 5 days of travel. Your tracking page shows the dates for each part.

Returns and refunds

Can I return something?

Each shop sets its own return window, shown on its product pages. Start a return with "Help with this order" on your tracking page. See Refunds and returns.

When do I get my money back?

If you paid online and a shop rejects or cancels its part, we refund exactly that part straight away. Banks can take a few working days to show it.

Payments

How can I pay?

Online by UPI or card, or cash on delivery when it is switched on. For cash on delivery we send a code to your phone first, so shops know the order is real.

Is paying online safe?

Payments are handled by the payment company. We never see or keep your card number, and an order only counts as paid once we have checked the payment company's answer.

Shops and reviews

How are shops checked?

Our team checks every new shop before it can sell, and can suspend shops that break the rules. You can report any listing from its product page.

Are the reviews real?

Only buyers whose order was delivered can review that product, once per order. Every review is marked "Verified buyer", and shops can reply once.
