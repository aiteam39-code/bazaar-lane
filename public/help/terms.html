Terms of use

These terms apply when you use Bazaar Lane to buy or to sell.

The marketplace

Bazaar Lane is a marketplace. Each product is sold by the shop named on it, not by Bazaar Lane. The shop is responsible for its product, its description, its stock, packing and sending it, and its returns.

Buying

Prices and shipping are set by each shop and shown before you pay. An order with several shops is split into one part per shop; each part is a separate sale with that shop. A shop may reject a part it cannot send; if you paid online, that part is refunded.

Selling

Shops must follow the seller guidelines. Bazaar Lane takes the commission shown on the Sell with us page on each delivered order and pays the rest to the shop. We may hide listings or suspend shops that break the rules.

Reviews and reports

Reviews must be honest and about the product. We may hide reviews that are abusive or not about the product.

Problems

Use "Help with this order" on your tracking page. If you and the shop cannot agree, our team decides, and can cancel and refund a part that has not been shipped.
