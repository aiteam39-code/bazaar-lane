Refunds and returns

Who handles what

  • Shops pack and send their own products, and handle returns within their own return window (shown on every product page).
  • Bazaar Lane refunds online payments when a shop rejects or cancels its part of your order: exactly that part's total, straight away.
  • Problems and disputes go through "Help with this order" on your tracking page. The shop answers, and our team decides if you cannot agree.

Returning a product

Open "Help with this order" for that shop's part, say what is wrong, and the shop will tell you how to send it back. Products from shops that say "No returns" cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or are not what was described.

Damaged or wrong products

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery through "Help with this order", with what happened. The shop replaces or refunds it.

Cash on delivery

If a cash-on-delivery part is cancelled before it ships, you pay nothing for it.
