How it works

For buyers

  1. Find something you like: search, open a category, or browse a shop.
  2. Add it to your bag. Your bag can hold products from many shops. Each shop shows its own shipping fee and when it ships free.
  3. Check out once as a guest: pay online, or choose cash on delivery (we check your phone with a code first).
  4. Follow your order on your private tracking link. Each shop packs and sends its own part, so parts can arrive on different days.
  5. Leave a review once a part is delivered, and ask for help from the tracking page if anything goes wrong.

For sellers

  1. Sign up with your shop name, email and phone.
  2. Get approved by our team, usually within a day.
  3. Add your products with photos, prices and stock, and set your shipping and returns.
  4. Fulfil orders: accept, pack, ship with a tracking number, and mark delivered.
  5. Get paid: each delivered order is booked on your Earnings page, less our commission, and paid out to you.
